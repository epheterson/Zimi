The licence · The manual page

Credit

The French deck’s jacks, queens and kings are from Adrian Kennard’s SVG playing cards (me.uk/cards), released into the public domain under CC0 1.0, recoloured to the deck’s palette. The court designs follow the 19th-century Goodall & Son pattern.

The Spanish deck’s sota, caballo and rey are from the Spanish-suited pack printed by B. P. Grimaud in 1860, as scanned by the Bibliothèque nationale de France (Gallica) and published on Wikimedia Commons. A 19th-century print, in the public domain; cropped, with the printed corner numbers painted out in the paper’s own colour.

The Hanafuda cards are Louie Mantia’s SVG Hanafuda from Wikimedia Commons, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. Changes: the paper backing and clip removed, the paths rounded, the whites, blacks, reds and ambers set to the deck’s palette. The adapted art is shared under the same licence.

The card indexes (A, 2 to 10, J, Q, K) are the outlines of Barlow Semi Condensed ExtraBold, Copyright 2017 The Barlow Project Authors (github.com/jpt/barlow), licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. The outlines are cut from the font and set as drawings; no font file is shipped, and two-digit ranks are narrowed to fit.
