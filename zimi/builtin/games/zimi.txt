Title: Games · Zimi
Description: Over 100 games from around the world, offline: cards, boards, arcade, puzzles
LongDescription: ZimiGames is a shelf of over 100 games that runs from this file in a web browser, with no network: cards (Solitaire, FreeCell, Hearts and more), board games (Chess, Checkers, Go, Shogi, Xiangqi and more), Simon Tatham's logic puzzles, arcade, word and number games, and ZimCity, a city builder. The pages, scripts and art are all inside the ZIM. Scores, times and saved games stay in the browser. In Kiwix JS it needs ServiceWorker mode (in Configuration); without scripts the shelf shows a short note and the list of games. It also opens in kiwix-serve. Code: GPL-3.0-or-later (Zimi). Third-party parts keep their own licences, which ship inside the ZIM: Simon Tatham's Portable Puzzle Collection (MIT), micropolisJS and Micropolis (GPL-3.0 with the Micropolis Public Name License), Fairy-Stockfish compiled to WebAssembly (GPL-3.0, source and patch included), Boxoban levels (Apache-2.0), card art (CC0, public domain, CC BY-SA 4.0) and Barlow outlines (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
