Yacht

Score card. Tap a row in your column to score it.

Roll up to 3 times a turn, keeping the dice you tap (or keys 1 to 5). Space rolls. Then score one open row. Three of a kind, Four of a kind, Full house and Choice score the total of all 5 dice. The straights score 30 and a Yacht 50. A total of 63 or more in the top 6 rows earns 35.
