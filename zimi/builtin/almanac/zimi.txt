Title: Almanac · Zimi
Description: Explore the sky, your planet and the universe, for any place and day, offline
LongDescription: The Almanac is an astronomy reference that runs from this file in a web browser, with no network, for any place and day: Moon phase, Sun and Moon rise and set, a live sky of stars and planets, the solar system, a 3D Earth, Moon and planets with the satellites over them, tides, calendars and tables. Every figure is computed in the page; the data it needs is inside the ZIM. This is the standard edition, without the high-resolution map tiles. In Kiwix JS it needs ServiceWorker mode (in Configuration); without scripts it shows a short note. It also opens in kiwix-serve. Code: GPL-3.0-or-later (Zimi). Data and libraries keep their own licences, listed in earth/SOURCES.txt inside the ZIM: NASA imagery and the Yale Bright Star Catalogue (public domain), ESO Milky Way panorama (CC BY 4.0), three.js and satellite.js (MIT), d3-celestial (BSD 3-Clause), Temml (MIT), CelesTrak satellite elements (a snapshot) and NOAA tide data (a snapshot). Voyager Golden Record pictures (golden-record/credits.json): all 49 shown, each with its credit. 8 are NASA photographs (public domain); the other 41 are copyright their owners: Jon Lomberg, UN Photo, Frank Drake, the National Astronomy and Ionosphere Center, Jay M. Pasachoff and Picturepoint, London.
