# 1.8.2 spot-check list — everything changed since 1.8.1 shipped

Grouped by where you'd look. Each line: what changed, where to click, what
"right" looks like.

## Bookmarks (the centerpiece — most new surface, check hardest)

Open the History/Bookmarks panel.

1. **Nested folders** — create a folder, create a folder inside it. Expect:
   inline input (no browser prompt), Enter commits, Esc cancels, collapsible.
2. **Drag to organize** — drag a bookmark into a folder; drag a folder into
   another folder. Expect: drop indicator, reparent persists after reload.
   *(Folder-into-folder drag has the weakest test coverage — push on it.)*
3. **Right-click / long-press** a bookmark AND a folder → Move to…, Rename,
   Delete. Deleting a folder should ask what happens to its contents.
4. **Export** — the export modal shows a folder TREE with checkboxes; pick any
   mix of top-level and sub-folders. Expect one ZIM per selected top-level
   folder, subfolders as sections inside.
5. **Export quality (the 1.8.0 complaint)** — open a generated ZIM. Articles
   should carry their real IMAGES and STYLING, not bare text.
6. **Where it lands** — the generated ZIM appears in your library/home.
   *(Download/Share buttons on it were pulled to 1.9 — not expected here.)*
7. **Signed-in vs anonymous** — signed in, folders live server-side; anonymous,
   in the browser.

## Almanac Time Machine

8. **Position** — the instrument now docks at the BOTTOM.
9. **No scroll bump** — scroll to the middle of the almanac, summon it (tap the
   hero clock). Expect: the page does NOT jump. (Measured 0px, verify by eye.)
10. **Crystal lever** — a translucent faceted amber disc on a brass shaft, slow
    facet spin. Drag feel should be unchanged from before.
11. **Live while scrubbing** — drag the lever and watch: date, moon
    illumination %, moon age, moon distance, sunrise, sunset, daylight length
    all update live. (Calendar grid / orrery / meteors settle on release —
    intentional, they're too heavy per frame.)
12. **Holidays** — Worldwide scope shows "· France", not "· FR".

## Library / manage

13. **Settings gear during load (#44)** — restart the server and click the gear
    IMMEDIATELY while the library is still loading. Expect: manage opens right
    away (it used to be dead until the scan finished).
14. **Filter row** — Manage → Library pill row now has ONLY category filters.
    Collection pills and the Reorder ↗ link are gone (Collections has its own
    tab; reorder lives in Settings → Library).

## Reader

15. **Broken video** — open a video article in a broken-scrape ZIM (your
    ted_en_technology). Expect a neutral "This video isn't included in this
    ZIM" box instead of a dead frame. A healthy video (your 2020-playlist TED
    ZIM) must still play with NO box.
16. **Math in dark mode** — open a Wikipedia article with formulas, app dark,
    darken-articles on. Formulas should be WHITE and legible (they were black
    on black — this was a real bug the verification pass caught). Photos in the
    same article must still look normal, not inverted.
17. **Image captions in light** — light theme + reader, an article with
    captioned images. Captions legible (were dark-on-dark).

## Home

18. **Padding** — a little more breathing room under the compact/full view
    toggle.

## Downloads (issue #46 — fix in progress at time of writing)

19. **Button sizing + overflow** — start a download and watch the panel:
    buttons should share one size system and nothing should escape its box, at
    phone width and desktop, in both themes.
