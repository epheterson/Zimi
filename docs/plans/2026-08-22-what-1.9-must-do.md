# What 1.9 must do

One page. Written 2026-08-22 after a week where I repeatedly said "fixed" and Eric opened it and found things.

## The goal

**A person types a URL, waits, and gets an offline copy that looks like the page they saw.** Everything else in this release is in service of that or can wait.

## Done means Eric-done, not Claude-done

The rule that was missing all week: **a thing is fixed when it is fixed in the web UI, on prod, on a phone, looked at with eyes.** Not when a test passes. Not when a harness prints a good number. My instruments lied three separate times this week — reporting a login wall as a clean capture, inventing 576 srcset fragments, counting not-yet-lazy-loaded images as broken — and each time I believed them over the person using the product.

So, per fix, before it is called done:
1. Do it through the **web UI on prod**, the way Eric would.
2. Look at the **screenshot**, do not just read the counters.
3. Say what was verified and what was not. "Tests pass" is not verification of anything a user sees.

## The five things that stand between here and shipping

Ranked by what a person hits first.

| # | What | State |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Size estimate lies.** Preview says 5.58 MB, ZIM is 36.3 MB — 6.5× off. It shows the HTML byte count, not an archive estimate. | broken, was marked fixed |
| 2 | **Black bar across the top of a capture.** An empty fixed ad slot whose ad was blocked. Reads as a missing hero image — this is what "missing top images" means. | diagnosed, never fixed |
| 3 | **No favicon or title on the create screen.** The ZIM has a correct icon; the create flow never shows one, so a 90-second wait shows nothing identifying. | never built |
| 4 | **Alive empties out on scroll.** 46/49 images on arrival, 0/49 after scrolling apple.com. Four fixes tried and reverted. Images at the TOP go dark too, so it is not a lazy-loader fetching new files — something resets every image. | unsolved, cause not found |
| 5 | **Fonts escape the ZIM.** `/fonts/cnn/...` resolves to the server root, 404s. Same class as #4's root-relative problem. | known, small |

## What is actually solid

Verified on prod, in the reader, with pictures: **Fast and Rendered both produce good CNN captures.** Rendered was 74/74 images. Fast renders masthead, hero, headline, article images. Site mode captures cleanly and says when it stops. Video fails with a sentence instead of a traceback. Deletes stick. The definition tip stopped nagging. Consent walls are gone from Rendered.

## The call to make

1, 2, 3 are small and are what a person meets in the first thirty seconds. 4 is a real unsolved bug in one engine.

**Ship 1.9 when 1, 2 and 3 are done and verified through the UI.** Alive ships with an honest sentence, or ships in 1.9.1 — Eric's call, but it should not hold 1 through 3 hostage, and neither should it be quietly described as equal to the other two.

## What I will stop doing

- Saying "prod-verified" when I mean "my harness printed a number".
- Marking a task complete without opening the thing in a browser.
- Chasing a fifth hypothesis when four have failed — that is the point to say so and hand it over.
