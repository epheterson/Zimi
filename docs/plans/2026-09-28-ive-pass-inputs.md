# 1.12 Jony Ive pass: inputs

What the UX pass found needs rethinking rather than fixing, collected per surface, plus Eric's read ("The UI has a lot, not saying it's bad but not sure it's the inevitable intuitive simple design we want"). The Ive pass (jony-ive-review) takes each surface in turn: its one job, hierarchy, inevitability, order, care in the unseen states, honesty.

## Bookshelf and ZimiTube (UX pass, media)

1. On a mixed shelf, each source could be a row of covers rather than a tile.
2. LibreTexts, Wikisource, Wikibooks and whole-ZIM books open as raw pages, not in the e-reader; PDFs get PDF.js's desktop toolbar on a phone.
3. Audio sits on a 16:9 video stage on desktop.
4. The Apps page counts entries, not books or videos, and lists ZIMs that yield nothing.
5. The 44px fact rows make a phone book page long.
6. LibreTexts lists "Back Matter" as a book.

## Almanac (UX pass)

1. The Earth view opens at the orrery's spun-forward date (the orrery runs fast by default), so a first open is rarely "live".
2. A partial solar eclipse shows nothing on the globe.
