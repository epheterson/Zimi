## Decisions (Eric, 2026-09-30)
- Like and Save are separate: the heart records a like and feeds Liked; it never adds a bookmark. Save is its own button.
- Download names: `Zimi-<ver>-<os>-<arch>`: `mac-arm64.dmg`, `mac-x64.dmg`, `windows-x64.zip`, `windows-x64-setup.exe`, `linux-x64.snap`, and `Zimi-<ver>-x86_64.AppImage` (the AppImage catalog's rule).
- Text size: five named steps (Smaller, Small, Default, Large, Larger), shown as growing A's, no numbers.
